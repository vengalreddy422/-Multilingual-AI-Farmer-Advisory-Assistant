📄 1. SHORT ABSTRACT (FOR HACKATHON SUBMISSION FORMS / PPT SLIDES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Title: Kisan Mitra: Autonomous Multilingual AI Agricultural Advisory & Crop Defense System

Abstract:
India's 140 million smallholder farmers face severe productivity losses due to 
delayed foliar disease diagnosis, linguistic barriers, toxic chemical overdosing, 
and poor digital connectivity. Kisan Mitra is an autonomous, decentralized 
agricultural operating system delivering precision agronomic intelligence in 6 Indian 
languages (Telugu, Hindi, English, Tamil, Kannada, Marathi). The platform unites 
multimodal Generative AI (Gemini 2.5 Flash), on-device edge neural vision (MobileNetV2 
via ONNX Runtime with <15ms latency), real-time crowdsourced spatial GPS pest outbreak 
telemetry, and an offline GSM SMS/IVR engine for 2G keypad phones. Integrated with 
CIB&RC chemical safety guardrails, FAO-56 evapotranspiration irrigation modeling, 
and live APMC mandi price indices, Kisan Mitra generates official 2-Page Master Farm 
Health Dossiers (PDF) and automatically dispatches them via live SMTP, transforming 
scientific agricultural research into accessible, safe, and zero-internet-ready 
decisions for rural India.

📄 2. EXTENDED TECHNICAL ABSTRACT (FOR PROJECT REPORT & JUDGING DOSSIER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Title: Kisan Mitra: A Decentralized Multimodal AI Platform for Precision Agronomy, 
Epidemiological Pest Surveillance, and Rural Zero-Connectivity Delivery

Abstract:
Agricultural advisory systems in developing nations often fail due to three fundamental 
bottlenecks: high digital illiteracy, lack of high-speed 4G connectivity in remote 
fields, and the dangerous hallucination of banned chemicals by generic LLMs. To resolve 
these challenges, we present Kisan Mitra—an integrated, multi-tier agricultural AI 
platform developed under Problem Statement I-NXS-010. 

Kisan Mitra features a conversational voice-to-voice AI agronomist grounded in ICAR 
standards and guarded by an autonomous CIB&RC (Central Insecticides Board & Registration 
Committee) safety engine that intercepts banned pesticides and caps foliar chemical 
concentrations (<15 ml/L) with mandatory Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI). For rapid disease 
detection, the system incorporates an optimized edge neural network (MobileNetV2) 
quantized with ONNX Runtime, diagnosing 38+ plant pathologies on standard CPU hardware 
in under 15 milliseconds. 

To prevent regional crop devastation, diagnosed incidents are geotagged in a relational 
SQLite ledger (`farm_ledger.db`), triggering Haversine spatial proximity algorithms 
that alert neighboring farmers within a 60 km radius through an interactive Pest 
Outbreak Radar. For the 60%+ rural demographic operating basic feature phones without 
internet, the system deploys a GSM 03.38-compliant SMS parser and automated IVR voice 
callback simulator delivering compact <=160 character prescriptions. 

Furthermore, the architecture embeds mathematical engines for FAO-56 Penman-Monteith 
evapotranspiration water budgeting, stoichiometric soil NPK balancing, live APMC mandi 
pricing, and automated 2-page Master Farm Dossier PDF compilation with real-time SMTP 
dispatch. Kisan Mitra represents a scalable, enterprise-grade, and resilient bridge 
between cutting-edge AI and grassroots agricultural empowerment.
